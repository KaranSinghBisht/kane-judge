# Check out with a card

## Put a notebook in the cart
Open http://localhost:4173, click "Add to cart" on the Field Notebook card, then click the Cart link in the header. Verify the checkout page lists the Field Notebook.

## Review the order summary
Verify the order summary shows a Subtotal of $18.00, Shipping of $5.00 and a Total of $23.00.

## Pay
Fill in Full name "Ada Lovelace", Email "ada@example.com", Card number "4242 4242 4242 4242", Expiry "12/30" and CVC "123", then click Pay. Verify the page says "Order confirmed" with an order number that starts with INK- and a total charged of $23.00.
