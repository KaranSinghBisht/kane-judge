# Browse the shop and fill the cart

## See the products
Open http://localhost:4173 and verify the product grid shows the Field Notebook for $18.00 and the Brass Pen for $42.00.

## Add two products to the cart
Click "Add to cart" on the Field Notebook card, then click "Add to cart" on the Brass Pen card. Verify the cart badge in the header shows 2.
