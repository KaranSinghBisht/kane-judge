# Check out and get an order number

## Open checkout with an item
Add any one product to the cart, then go to the checkout page. Verify the checkout page visibly lists the product you added with its price.

## Read the order summary
Look at the order summary on the checkout page. Verify it visibly shows a Subtotal equal to the listed items' prices, Shipping of $5.00, and a Total equal to the Subtotal plus $5.00.

## Pay by card
Fill in the payment form with test data: the name "Kane Judge test", the email test+kanejudge@example.com, the card number 4242 4242 4242 4242, an expiry date in the future such as 12/30 and the CVC 123, plus obvious test values for any other required field. Place the order. Verify the page visibly shows an order number.
