# Checkout lists the items and an order summary

## Put a product in the cart
Add the first product in the catalogue to the cart. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows a count of at least 1.

## Open checkout
Open the cart and go to checkout. Verify the checkout page visibly lists the product you added, with its name and price.

## Read the order summary
Look at the order summary on the checkout page. Verify the order summary visibly shows a Subtotal, a Shipping amount of $5, and a Total that equals the Subtotal plus $5.
