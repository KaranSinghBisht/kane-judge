# Browse the catalogue and add a product to the cart

## Browse the catalogue
Open the shop's home page and look at the product catalogue. Verify the page visibly shows four products, each with its name and a price.

## Add a product to the cart
Add the first product in the catalogue to the cart. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows a count of 1.
