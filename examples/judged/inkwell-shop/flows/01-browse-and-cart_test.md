# Browse the catalogue and add items to the cart

## Browse the catalogue
Open the shop's home page and look at the catalogue. Verify the page visibly lists four products, each with a name and a price.

## Add a product to the cart
Add the first product to the cart. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows 1.

## Add a second product
Add a different product to the cart. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows 2.
