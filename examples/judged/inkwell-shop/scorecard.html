Summary

A small stationery shop whose storefront, cart and card checkout work end to end and return an order number. Two of the four README features fail in the live demo: once SAVE10 is applied the order summary shows the discount but no Total (a client-side experiment running at 100% rollout deletes the fourth summary row), and the promised Account → Orders history does not exist (no Account page, no link to one, and orders are never stored).

Works5/10The core purchase loop works (c1 and c2 proven: browse, cart badge, summary, card checkout, order number), but two of four claims are broken: SAVE10 leaves the order summary without a Total (c3) and Account → Orders does not exist (c4).
Substance5/10There is real server-side logic: the cart is re-priced on the server with quantity and promo validation, and the promo discount is computed there. But orders are never stored (createOrder returns a random id), the card fields are never read or validated, and there is no account area (c4).
Truthfulness4/10Two of four feature bullets are wrong: the promo bullet promises a new total that the page hides (c3), and the order-history bullet describes a page that does not exist (c4). The other two bullets are accurate (c1, c2), and the README is honest that no payment is taken; checkout.js posts only name, email, items and promo code, so card details do stay in the browser.
Craft6/10The storefront is clean and coherent and the badge, pricing and confirmation behave well (c1, c2). The rough edge is serious, though: the checkout hides what you will pay as soon as a promo is applied (console: "[experiments] compact-order-summary: limit-rows removed 1 of 4 rows"), and a placed order can never be looked up.
Originality3/10A stock webshop demo (catalogue, cart, promo code, checkout); the zero-dependency Node setup is tidy but the idea is a familiar template with no distinctive twist.
