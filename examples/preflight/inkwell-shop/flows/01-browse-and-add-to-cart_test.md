# Browse the catalogue and add products to the cart

## Browse the catalogue
Open the shop's home page and look at the product list. Verify the page visibly lists four products, each shown with a name and a price in dollars, and that the cart badge in the header visibly shows 0.

## Add a product to the cart
Add the "Brass Pen" to the cart without leaving the page. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows 1.

## Add a second product
Add the "Desk Mat" to the cart as well. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows 2.
