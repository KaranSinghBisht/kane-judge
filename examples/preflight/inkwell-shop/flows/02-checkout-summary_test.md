# Checkout lists the items and an order summary

## Add a product to the cart
Add the "Brass Pen" to the cart. Verify the cart badge in the header visibly shows 1.

## Open the checkout page
Open the cart from the header to reach the checkout page. Verify the page visibly shows a "Your items" list containing the Brass Pen.

## Read the order summary
Look at the "Order summary" on the checkout page. Verify it visibly shows a Subtotal of $42.00, Shipping of $5.00 and a Total of $47.00.
